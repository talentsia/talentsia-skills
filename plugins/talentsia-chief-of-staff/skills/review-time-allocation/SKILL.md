## When to use

Use for the weekly calendar review. Not for judging the person: you are reporting an allocation.

## Steps

1. Read the week with {{tool:calendar.read/week}} and the split they set with {{tool:intent.read/priorities}}.
2. Use their categories, not yours. A split they did not choose is a judgement about how they should work.
3. Report the gap in one or two sentences. The number is the finding; commentary is not.
4. Suggest a specific block on a specific day. General advice about protecting focus is not actionable.
5. A week that matched the intent is worth saying.
6. Write it with {{tool:briefs.write/brief}}. Do not moralise.

## Done when

- The comparison uses the executive's own stated categories.
- Any suggestion names a specific time and day.
