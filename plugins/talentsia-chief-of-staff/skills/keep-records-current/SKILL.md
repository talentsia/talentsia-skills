name: keep-records-current
description: "Turn finished work into the promises and priorities it implies, without inventing anything it did not say."
---

## When to use

Use after a piece of work is finished, when its document may name promises, dates or a new priority. Recording nothing is a real outcome when the work implies nothing.

## Steps

1. Read what is already recorded with {{tool:commitments.read/list}} and {{tool:intent.read/priorities}} before writing anything.
2. Pull every promise out of the work with {{agent:extract-promises}}: who owes it, what, when in the work's own words, and what finished looks like.
3. Compare them with what is recorded using {{agent:match-existing}}. A promise entered twice is worse than one entered late.
4. Record each new promise with {{tool:commitments.write/record}}, against one owner. Two names is nobody: record two promises.
5. A missing date never stops a promise being recorded. Where the work gives a date, set it with {{tool:commitments.write/date}}; words like 'by Week 2' stay in the title.
6. Record a promise even when nobody here can keep it, and say so. Never hand it to whoever is available: then it stops looking like a gap.
7. Propose a priority with {{tool:intent.propose/propose}} only when the work shows something the list does not cover, with the evidence.
8. Recording nothing is a real outcome. A plan that schedules work and names nobody is not that: report it as unowned.

## Done when

- Every commitment recorded names one owner and traces to something the work actually said.
- No priority was created, only proposed with its evidence.
- Nothing was recorded that is already in the existing commitments or priorities.
- Every promise the work names was recorded, including the ones with no date.
- Work that sets out actions with no owner was reported as unowned rather than passed over.
- Work needing somebody nobody employs was recorded and reported as such, not reassigned.

## Notes

- What the organisation cares about is the executive's to decide; a coordinator proposes and never sets it.
- What the work could not establish, with who could answer each, is worth repeating: it is what unblocks the rest.
