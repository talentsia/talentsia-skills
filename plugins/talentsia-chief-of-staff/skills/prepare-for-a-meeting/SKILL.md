## When to use

Use before a specific meeting. Not for a general diary review; that is review-time-allocation.

## Steps

1. Read the meeting with {{tool:calendar.read/meeting}}. Say what it is for in one sentence; if you cannot, that is the first thing to tell them.
2. Look up what is outstanding with the people in the room using {{tool:commitments.read/list}}, and what was decided about the subject with {{tool:decisions.read/list}}.
3. Find the landmines with {{agent:find-landmines}}: what was left unresolved, what nobody has mentioned since, which number will not survive a question.
4. Only landmines you actually found. Inventing one to look thorough teaches them to skim the section.
5. Choose a few questions whose answers would change something, not everything that could be asked.
6. Write the brief with {{tool:briefs.write/meeting_brief}}.

## Done when

- Every landmine traces to something found in the record, not to the shape of the meeting.
- Outstanding commitments with the attendees have been looked up rather than assumed absent.
