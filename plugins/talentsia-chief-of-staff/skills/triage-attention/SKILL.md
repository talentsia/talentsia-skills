## When to use

Use when mail or inquiries have arrived and are waiting to be judged. Not for answering them: a reply is prepared as a draft, never sent.

## Steps

1. Read what is waiting with {{tool:attention.read/inbox}}. Start from the assumption that it does not need them; what does will survive the question.
2. Judge each with {{agent:classify-message}}: what goes wrong if they never see it.
3. A decision only needs a decision if a person must choose between real options. Something that merely needs doing is work.
4. Name the specific next step. 'This asks for a date and the contract expires Friday' does the thinking; 'reply to this' does not.
5. Where you can prepare the response, draft it with {{tool:drafts.write/draft_reply}} and say so. Nothing is sent.
6. Work that belongs to a colleague's trade is handed over with {{tool:work.handoff/delegate}}, not done here.
7. Record every judgement, including what you filtered out, with {{tool:attention.write/record}}, so nothing is raised twice.

## Done when

- Anything marked as needing them carries a recommendation naming a specific next step.
- Things filed as not needing them are recorded, not silently dropped.
- Every message waiting when the scan began has a recorded judgement.

## Notes

- Reducing noise is not hiding it: monitoring is a pile on the screen, not a place messages vanish.
- A scan that records nothing while mail waits is unfinished, however reasonable its answer sounds.
