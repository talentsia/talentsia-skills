# Talentsia Chief of Staff

The coordinator's craft: briefing an executive, triaging what arrives, keeping promises recorded and closed, preparing meetings and reviewing where time went.

Free, basic edition for Talentsia Edge workers. MIT licensed. The contract these skills follow is [docs/edge-worker-skills.md](../../docs/edge-worker-skills.md); validate with `python3 scripts/check_edge_skills.py`.

| Skill | What it does | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| [`brief-the-executive`](skills/brief-the-executive/SKILL.md) | Turn a night of organisational activity into the three things that matter today and, at most, one warning. | `executive_briefing` v1 |
| [`triage-attention`](skills/triage-attention/SKILL.md) | Decide what deserves a person out of everything that has arrived, and record every judgement, including what was filtered out. | `attention_triage` v1 |
| [`keep-records-current`](skills/keep-records-current/SKILL.md) | Turn finished work into the promises and priorities it implies, without inventing anything it did not say. | `keeping_records` v4 |
| [`close-loops`](skills/close-loops/SKILL.md) | Make sure what people promised actually happens, without spending the executive on it. | `loop_closing` v1 |
| [`prepare-for-a-meeting`](skills/prepare-for-a-meeting/SKILL.md) | What somebody needs to know before walking into a particular room, including the landmines that were actually found. | `meeting_preparation` v1 |
| [`review-time-allocation`](skills/review-time-allocation/SKILL.md) | Compare where a week went against where it was meant to go, in the executive's own categories. | `time_allocation` v1 |
