# Methodology sources and attribution

Talentsia Do is an independent Talentsia implementation informed by David Allen's Getting Things Done (GTD) methodology and the book *Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity*. Talentsia does not claim ownership of that methodology or affiliation with or endorsement by David Allen or the David Allen Company. Product branding is Talentsia Do; references below identify the methodological sources.

The package contains operating instructions adapted from the existing reusable source, with assistant stewardship, authority boundaries, and record practices. It does not bundle the book or source PDFs. A link identifies a source; it does not grant permission to reproduce it.
