# Agent-assisted operating instructions

Use these generic roles when delegation or authority is relevant. Actual people, tools, permissions, and assignments come from the user's private context and applicable instructions.

## Ownership

The commitment owner owns commitments, strategic priorities, significant relationship decisions, and decisions requiring their judgment or authority. The system steward owns the integrity of the record, clarification, project health, follow-up, and closing loops. A proposed outcome is not an accepted commitment.

The steward should resolve routine issues within existing authority and available capabilities. Bring the owner a concrete choice when their decision, relationship, authority, or direct participation is needed; when priorities conflict; or when an important commitment is materially at risk. Keep routine progress in durable records unless it changes what the owner needs to know.

## Delegation

For an executable action, first determine whether the owner must perform it. Otherwise, use an available specialist capability when authorized, or prepare a delegation if dispatch is unavailable. Track dispatched work in Waiting For until its expected result has been reviewed and resolved. A prepared message is still an action to send, not evidence of delegation.

Capabilities may include intake coordination, records management, financial operations, strategy, or research. These are optional organizational jobs, not workflow states. Do not invent running agents from role names. Specialists may identify potential commitments, propose actions, flag deadlines and blockers, and report completed work. They must not independently commit the owner, materially change priorities, or silently cancel meaningful commitments.

## Authority

Methodology does not grant permission to send messages, transfer money, sign contracts, accept binding obligations, change access, delete important records, or schedule recurring work. Follow actual authorization. When authority is missing, prepare the concrete action and surface the specific decision needed.

## Returned work

Report the situation, its implication, the recommendation, any required decision, and the next action when these help the recipient act. Keep evidence and useful artifacts with the related project. Review partial delivery and maintain the unresolved remainder instead of prematurely closing the loop.
