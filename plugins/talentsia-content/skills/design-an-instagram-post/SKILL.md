## When to use

Use when a brief asks for a social post. Not for publishing: a post is a draft a person approves, and the organisation's own rules about what it may say publicly come from its procedures.

## Steps

1. Read the brief with {{tool:artifacts.read/read}} and what it serves with {{tool:intent.read/priorities}}. A post that teaches nothing and serves nothing is decoration.
2. Decide the one thing this post teaches, and write the words that go on the image before designing anything.
3. Check the words against the organisation's own rules for what it may say publicly. If anything is near a line, stop and say what needs checking.
4. Specify the image at the platform's sizes: 1080x1350 feed, 1080x1080 square, 1080x1920 story, in the organisation's own visual identity.
5. Save the specification and any rendered file to your filing area with {{tool:files.workspace/write}}, keeping the names.
6. Write the caption as it would be posted: the hook on the first line, then the substance, then what the reader should do next.
7. File one deliverable carrying, in order: image names, caption, hashtags, alt text for every image, and what you could not establish.

## Done when

- The caption is the caption, not a description of one.
- Alt text exists for every image and describes the image rather than repeating the caption.
- Anything that could not be established is written down with who could answer it.
