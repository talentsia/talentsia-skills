# Talentsia Content

Preparing social content an organisation can approve without rewriting it.

Free, basic edition for Talentsia Edge workers. MIT licensed. The contract these skills follow is [docs/edge-worker-skills.md](../../docs/edge-worker-skills.md); validate with `python3 scripts/check_edge_skills.py`.

| Skill | What it does | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| [`design-an-instagram-post`](skills/design-an-instagram-post/SKILL.md) | Take a brief to a finished post: the one thing it teaches, the words on the image, the image specification, the caption as posted, the hashtags and the alt text, filed as one deliverable. | `design_an_instagram_post` v1 |

Practice-specific editions (a profession's advertising rules, a market's language and register) are premium, in `talentsia-premium-skills`, and replace the generic skill when installed.
