# Agent-assisted operating instructions

Use these generic roles when delegation or authority is relevant. Actual people, tools, permissions and assignments come from the user's private context and applicable workspace instructions, never from this package.

## Ownership

The <owner role, for example commitment owner, account holder, decision-maker> owns <the decisions this domain turns on>, strategic priorities, significant relationship decisions and anything requiring their judgement or authority. The <steward role, for example system steward, record keeper> owns the integrity of the record, clarification, follow-up and closing loops. A proposed <outcome> is not an accepted one.

The steward resolves routine issues within existing authority and available capabilities. It brings the owner a concrete choice when their decision, relationship, authority or direct participation is needed; when priorities conflict; or when an important <commitment or obligation> is materially at risk. Routine progress stays in durable records unless it changes what the owner needs to know.

## Delegation

For an executable step, first determine whether the owner must perform it. Otherwise use an available specialist capability when authorized, or prepare a delegation when dispatch is unavailable. Track dispatched work until its expected result has been reviewed and resolved. A prepared message is still an action to send, not evidence of delegation.

Specialist capabilities are optional organisational jobs, not workflow states, and a role name does not imply a running agent. Specialists may identify potential <commitments>, propose actions, flag deadlines and blockers, and report completed work. They must not independently commit the owner, materially change priorities or silently cancel anything meaningful.

## Authority

Method grants no permission to <send messages, transfer money, sign, accept binding obligations, change access, delete important records, publish, schedule recurring work>. Follow actual authorization for the specific instance. When authority is missing, prepare the concrete action and surface the specific decision needed.

## Returned work

Report the situation, its implication, the recommendation, any required decision and the next action when these help the recipient act. Keep evidence and useful artifacts with the related <project or object>. Review partial delivery and maintain the unresolved remainder instead of prematurely closing the loop.
