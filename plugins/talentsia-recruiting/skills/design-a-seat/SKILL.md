## When to use

Use once a diagnosis has shown a seat is the answer. Not for every vacancy: a reshuffle may fill it.

## Steps

1. Write what the seat is for in two sentences a stranger could act on. A title with a model behind it answers every question the same way.
2. Name the kinds of work it accepts, from the work this device hosts; check with {{tool:team.read/roster}}. Work nothing here defines is a trade to build.
3. Name what it may hold, and nothing beyond what the work needs.
4. Say what must exist before anybody could fill it: an account, a credential, a licence, a qualification.
5. Say how its first piece of work will be judged, and make that the work that was blocked.
6. Say whether it thinks on this device or on the platform, and why: a seat that must act needs tools.
7. Put the design in the request with {{tool:staffing.request/request}}.

## Done when

- The seat names a checkable first piece of work that is already blocked.
- Everything it may hold is justified by the work named.
- What must exist before it can be filled is stated, or explicitly nothing.
