description: "Turn promises nobody can keep into a named gap per area, and say which of them is actually a seat."
---

## When to use

Use when work is stuck across the organisation and somebody asks whether to hire. Most answers are cheaper than a person.

## Steps

1. Read what is stuck before proposing anything: {{tool:commitments.mine/list}}, {{tool:team.read/gaps}} and {{tool:artifacts.read/list}}.
2. Read who already works here and what each takes with {{tool:team.read/roster}}. A seat that exists is not a gap.
3. Group what is stuck by the area accountable for it. Two promises blocked on the same missing thing are one gap.
4. Name each group as one of: missing information, a missing written procedure, a missing tool, not enough capacity, or a trade nobody here practises.
5. Only capacity and a missing trade are a seat. For the other three, name the cheaper remedy.
6. Check the same seat has not already been requested: two requests for one gap are two people waiting on one decision.

## Done when

- Every stuck promise in the diagnosis names one of the five gaps in words.
- No seat is proposed for a gap that is not capacity or a missing trade.
- Nothing is claimed about who works here that the roster did not return.
