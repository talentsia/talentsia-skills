## When to use

Use when a diagnosed gap is a seat and a person has to approve it. One prepared request protects their attention; five do not.

## Steps

1. Check what is already waiting with {{tool:team.read/gaps}}: an open request for the same gap is two people waiting on one decision.
2. Ask for one seat at a time with {{tool:staffing.request/request}}, carrying its mission, its gap, what it needs and its procedures.
3. Say in the request why no existing position can own the work, naming the positions you ruled out. A request without that is a wish.
4. Where the gap is not a seat, prepare it as a decision with {{tool:decisions.prepare/raise}} or say it plainly on the promise.
5. Record on each blocked promise what you did about it, with {{tool:commitments.mine/update}}.
6. Never say a seat is filled or a worker is working until the tool that does it returned success in this job.

## Done when

- One request per seat, each carrying its gap, its needs and its procedures.
- Every blocked promise the request came from carries a note saying so.
- No claim of a filled seat without a tool result behind it.
