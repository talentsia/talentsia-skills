description: "Write the steps that turn a seat into a worker that does the work the same way twice."
---

## When to use

Use when a new seat needs its method, or a missing procedure is the gap. What you write is the organisation's own procedure; it never becomes a public skill.

## Steps

1. Start from the blocked work, not from the title: the evidence for a procedure is the piece of work that needed it.
2. Write steps in the imperative, each an act somebody could take and somebody else could check.
3. Put reading before deciding: name the tool that establishes each fact a later step relies on.
4. Name only tools the seat will actually hold; check with {{tool:team.read/roster}}.
5. Write what finishing looks like as its own short list, checkable from records rather than from the worker's account.
6. Keep it to eight steps. A procedure longer than that is more than one procedure.
7. Carry the procedure with the staffing request, {{tool:staffing.request/request}}, so one approval creates the worker and its method.

## Done when

- Every step is an act, and every fact a later step relies on is established by a named tool earlier.
- No step requires a tool the seat does not hold.
- Finishing is stated in terms that can be checked from records.
- The procedure has at most eight steps.
