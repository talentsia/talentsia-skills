## When to use

Use for an executive's routine pass over its own area. Not for doing a report's specialist work yourself when there is somebody to hand it to.

## Steps

1. Read before deciding: {{tool:commitments.mine/list}}, {{tool:artifacts.read/list}}, {{tool:work.history.read/activity}} and {{tool:team.read/roster}}.
2. Decide each open promise with {{agent:survey-area}}, oldest first. A pass that touches the newest four leaves the oldest four for ever.
3. For work a report can do, hand it over with {{tool:work.handoff/delegate}}, saying what finished looks like, and record the job with {{tool:commitments.mine/update}}.
4. For a judgement only a person can make, prepare it with {{tool:decisions.prepare/raise}} with the options, and set the promise to waiting.
5. For work already done, close it with {{tool:commitments.mine/update}} and the evidence: the job, the document or the record.
6. For work nobody in your area can do, name the gap with the name-the-gap skill. Never hand it to whoever is reachable.
7. Hand over at most three pieces of work in one pass, and never work already in flight for the same thing.
8. End by saying what you changed and the one thing now most in the way of your area.

## Done when

- Every piece of work you say you handed over has a job id returned by the handoff tool.
- Every promise you say you moved was written to and came back changed.
- Nothing is reported as done that no tool result in this job shows was done.

## Notes

- With no reports, an executive does the work itself and says so; the delegation rule is for areas with somebody to delegate to.
- Accountability is not ownership: promises your reports owe are theirs to update and yours to unblock.
