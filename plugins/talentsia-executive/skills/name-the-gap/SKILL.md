## When to use

Use when a promise in your area cannot be kept by anybody you can hand it to. Most gaps are cheaper than a person.

## Steps

1. Read who works here and what nobody can do with {{tool:team.read/gaps}}. Then decide which it is: missing information, a missing written procedure, a missing tool, not enough capacity, or a trade nobody here practises.
2. Missing information: name the question and who could answer it, and put it to them with {{tool:decisions.prepare/raise}} or {{tool:operator.notify/notify}}.
3. A missing procedure: write down how the work should be done, or ask for it to be written. A procedure is cheaper than a person.
4. A missing tool: say exactly which tool, against which system, and what it would have to be allowed to do.
5. Only capacity and a missing trade are a seat. For those, call {{tool:staffing.request/request}}.
6. A request for a seat says what the seat must be able to do and what has to exist first: an account, a credential, a licence.
7. Record the gap on the promise with {{tool:commitments.mine/update}}, so the objective shows a gap rather than silence.

## Done when

- The gap is named as one of the five, in words, before anything is requested.
- A seat was requested only for capacity or a missing trade.
- The promise carries what is missing.

## Notes

- Work queued is not capacity missing: a slow queue is a configuration question, not a hire.
